Why checkout stays on-site

Physical merch can't be delivered by a download link, so this store keeps browsing, cart, and checkout in one place, with printing and shipping handled behind the scenes.

Digital downloads can link out

A store selling digital files, like a PDF download, can send buyers to an outside checkout such as Payhip: the file is delivered instantly the moment someone pays, so nothing needs to be printed or shipped.

Common questions

Do I ever leave this website to pay?

No. You browse, add to cart, and check out all on 610 Supply Co.'s site. That is the difference from a digital-download store, where the button sends you elsewhere to pay and download a file.

Is there a shopping cart on this site?

Yes. Tap Add to cart on any product and the cart drawer slides in on this page. You can change quantities, remove items, and check out from there, still without leaving the site.

When is my shirt made, and who makes it?

After you pay at checkout here, your order flows automatically to Printify. Printify prints the item for that order, then ships it to you. Nothing sits in inventory beforehand; that is the print-on-demand, no-stock model.

Why not use Payhip here?

Because Payhip delivers digital files, and a T-shirt is not a file. It cannot print or ship physical merch. For physical products, the checkout has to connect to a print partner, and here that partner is Printify, working behind the scenes.
